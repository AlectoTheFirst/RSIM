# `rsim-inventory`

`rsim-inventory` holds the inventory in PostgreSQL through `tokio-postgres`, with TLS through `rustls` and `ring` and the CA bundle of `RSIM_INVENTORY_CA_FILE`. The schema is the SQL files in `migrations/`, which `Db::migrate` applies in order under an advisory lock and records in `schema_migrations`; a reader or a writer refuses a database at another schema version.

The tables are the credentials (`credentials`: a name and the source `env` or `env-url` with its key, or the source `managed` with its kind `password`, `ssh-key` or `api-key`, its user, its secret sealed by ChaCha20-Poly1305 with the name and the kind as the associated data, the id of the key and the revision of the secret; `secret.rs` holds `SecretKey`, `SecretInput` and `Sealed`, `Db::load` gives no secret and only `Db::load_with_secrets` of the collector reads the sealed bytes), the jump hosts, the routes with their proxy URL with no login, the reference of the proxy login and the jump hosts in order (`route_jumps`), the device groups (the vendor and the settings), the devices (the name of the store, the address, the site, the location, the site of a console and the settings that override the group), the Equinix bindings (`fabric_port_bindings` and `edge_device_bindings`), the settings of the collector (`collection_settings`, one row: `history_days` from 1 to 3650, default 30, at `GET` and `PUT /v1/inventory/collection` and in `GET /v1/inventory`), `inventory_version`, `audit_log`, and the sign-in tables of `migrations/0002_auth.sql`: `users` (the role `viewer`, `operator` or `admin` and the Argon2id password hash), `sessions` and `api_tokens` (the SHA-256 hash of the token only), `login_failures` and `auth_events`.

`db/auth.rs` holds their reader and writer: a session stops 12 hours after its last use and 7 days after the sign-in, 50 failed sign-ins of one user name or 5 of one client address in 15 minutes give a wait, the database gives each time, and the last enabled admin cannot lose the role or be disabled or removed.

A setting of a device that is null inherits from its group, and a setting of a group that is null takes the default of `resolve.rs`: the credential, the route (none is a direct connection, and a route with no proxy and no jump host is a direct route that a device can set to override its group), the SSH algorithms, mode and channels, the NX-OS JSON form, the TLS trust and CA file, `capture_bgp`, the 4 time limits, the retries with 0 as the default, the HA setting `none`, `all` or `one` with `none` as the default, the collection mode `collection` `auto` or `manual` with `auto` as the default (migration `0009_manual_collection.sql`), the collection interval `interval_seconds` from 60 to 86400 with 60 as the default and 900 for the vendor `equinix` (`default_interval_seconds`, with the device time limit `default_timeout_seconds`), and the settings of a VeloCloud orchestrator (migration `0007_orchestrator.sql`): `orchestrator_release`, `api_base`, `concurrency` from 1 to 16 with 4 as the default and `refresh_seconds` from 0 to 86400 with 1800 as the default, the settings of an Equinix account (migration `0008_equinix.sql`): `network_edge` `off` or `on` with `off` as the default and `page_size` from 1 to 1000 with 100 as the default; the TLS trust with no setting is `public` for the vendors `velocloud` and `equinix` and `pin` for each other vendor (`default_tls_trust`), in `resolve.rs` and in `SETTING_FIELDS` of the UI; each resolved setting has its origin `device`, `group` or `default`. The collector does not use the HA setting and the time of a paged section yet; it gives the retries and the request time of an HTTPS device to the transport. The vendor of a group is a word, and a vendor with no collector is allowed.

`Db::apply` writes a list of changes in one transaction: a change that leaves its record as it is writes nothing, and otherwise the version increases by 1 and each changed record gets an audit record with the actor, the time of the database, the version, the kind, the key and the old and the new value.

`env.rs` reads the env file of the collector (`parse_env`) and imports it (`import`): one group for each vendor with the reference `env:<P>`, the route of the vendor keys, a route of the device for a device with its own `VIA` or `PROXY` key, the reference `env-url:<key>` for the login of a proxy URL, and for each `VELOCLOUD_<NAME>_URL` the group `velocloud-<name>` with the reference `env:VELOCLOUD_<NAME>`, its TLS trust, retries, time limits and orchestrator settings, and one device `vco-<name>-<logicalId>` for each enterprise with the logicalId as its console site, and for each `EQUINIX_<NAME>_URL` the group and the device `equinix-<name>` with the reference `env:EQUINIX_<NAME>` when the account has its own client, else `env:EQUINIX` (`env::equinix_client`); a client certificate of an orchestrator has no inventory setting, and the import refuses it. The binary `rsim-inventory` runs the migrations, the import and a print.

In `rsim-collect`, `setup_from_inventory` of the binary turns the inventory and the secrets of the env file into the setup of a cycle, and its test checks that an env file and its import give the same devices, routes, credentials, profiles and time limits. `Collector::reconfigure` sets the inventory, the sources and the time limits before a cycle, and `Inventory::version` gives the version that the snapshot records. A failed read of the inventory, a reference with no secret and an inventory with no device of a collector write no snapshot, so a typing error does not remove devices.

A device with the collection mode `manual` is never due by its interval: `POST /v1/inventory/devices/{device}/collect` and `POST /v1/inventory/groups/{group}/collect` ("Collect now" in the device panel and the group panel) write a row of `collection_requests` for each device and send `NOTIFY rsim_collect` (`Db::request_collection`); the collector with `--repeat` listens on that channel on a connection of its own (`Db::listen_collect`), takes the requests at each wake (`Db::take_collection_requests`) and collects each requested device at once with a forced collection (`Collector::request`, `Collector::set_manual`).

`rsim-api/src/inventory.rs` holds the routes `/v1/inventory`: `GET /v1/inventory/vendors`, the vendors of `rsim_inventory::COLLECTOR_VENDORS` with their transport, which needs no database and which a test of `rsim-collect` compares with `Vendor::ALL`, the GET routes of the inventory, the groups, the devices with the settings resolved, the credentials, the routes, the jump hosts, the bindings and the audit log, and the PUT and DELETE routes, which write with the actor `rsim-api`. The PUT body of a credential (`CredentialPut`) can hold the secret of a managed credential, which the server seals with the key of the env key `RSIM_SECRET_KEY`, in the environment or in the env file of `--env`, and no answer and no audit record gives back; a managed credential with no secret in the body keeps its stored secret when the kind stays, a new secret increases `secret_revision`, and with no key the server refuses a secret with 409 `no_secret_key`. `GET /v1/inventory/secret-key` gives whether the server has a key, with no id. Their OpenAPI scope is `rsim.inventory.write`. The SQL tests use a new schema for each test, from `rsim_inventory::test_database`.

## Commands

| Task | Command |
|---|---|
| Inventory tests | `cargo test -p rsim-inventory`. The SQL tests in `tests/pg.rs` and in `rsim-api/tests/inventory.rs` read `RSIM_TEST_DATABASE_URL`; with it unset they print that they are skipped and pass. |
| Local database of the SQL tests | `/opt/podman/bin/podman run -d --rm --name rsim-pg -e POSTGRES_USER=rsim -e POSTGRES_PASSWORD=rsim-test -e POSTGRES_DB=rsim_test -p 127.0.0.1:5432:5432 docker.io/library/postgres:17.11`, then `RSIM_TEST_DATABASE_URL=postgres://rsim:rsim-test@127.0.0.1:5432/rsim_test cargo test --workspace`, and `/opt/podman/bin/podman stop rsim-pg` at the end. CI runs the same image as a service container. |
| Inventory database | `cargo run -p rsim-inventory -- --database <postgres URL> migrate`, `import-env <env file>`, `show --resolved`, or `user add <name> --role viewer|operator|admin` and `user password <name>` with the password on standard input, `user disable <name>` and `user list`. `RSIM_INVENTORY_URL` can give the URL and `RSIM_INVENTORY_CA_FILE` the CA bundle of the server for TLS. |
