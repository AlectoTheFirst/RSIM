# `rsim-topologies`

The hand-built topologies are in the crate `rsim-topologies`, with one module for each topology in `crates/rsim-topologies/src/`. They are test fixtures with the twin identifier `builtin.<topology>`: a command of `rsim-cli` with such a name needs `--features test-fixtures`. `layer2_site()` is a switched site behind a router for the layer 2 tests; it is not a built-in topology. The tests and the benchmarks of `rsim-engine` use it as a development dependency. `scale_estate(ScaleOpts)` in `rsim-topologies/src/scale.rs` is the generated estate of the scale benchmark, from a fixed pseudo-random sequence; it is not a built-in topology. `rsim-topologies::builtin(name)` gives the twin of a hand-built topology.

`leaf_spine()` is a VXLAN fabric of 4 leaves and 2 spines with symmetric routing in one tenant VRF; `rsim-functions/src/tunnel.rs` holds its tunnel ends `VxlanEncap` and `VxlanDecap`.

`sdwan_site(SdwanOpts)` in `rsim-topologies/src/sdwan_site.rs` is a branch edge and a hub edge of an SD-WAN with one segment and 2 tunnels, over the transports `internet` and `mpls`; it is not a built-in topology. The default route of the branch goes to its business policy, a `PolicyRoute` whose rule `backhaul-dns` sends the DNS packets to the hub and whose other packets leave direct on the public link; with `SdwanOpts::firewall`, each edge has an `AclMatch` for the packets from its LAN. `rsim-functions/src/sdwan.rs` holds the functions of the overlay. `TransportLookup` is the route lookup of a transport network outside the twin; each of its branches to an egress interface has the assumption that the network carries UDP 2426 from the ingress interface to the egress interface. `OverlaySelect` gives one candidate for each tunnel to a far end that is up; a steering rule with links keeps only the tunnels of its links, a steering rule with an empty link set keeps each tunnel, a rule of `OverlaySelect::mandatory` drops its packets when no tunnel of its links is up and another rule with links gives `Unknown` then, and a kept tunnel whose state is not collected gives `Unknown`. `VcmpEncap` puts the packets of a segment into the tunnel of one pair of WAN links and sets the WAN interface and the next hop of the outer packet. `VcmpDecap` takes the VCMP packets to a WAN link of the device out of the tunnel, and the segment selects the VRF.

`equinix_fabric()` in `rsim-topologies/src/equinix_fabric.rs` is the built-in topology `equinix-fabric`: the switch `sw-a` of site A, the switch `sw-b` of site B and the Equinix Fabric account `equinix-acct` with 3 circuits from the Fabric port `fp-a`: `evpl-100` with VLAN 100 at both ends, `evpl-qinq` from VLAN 200 to the QinQ port `fp-b2` with the outer VLAN 300, the TPID `0x88a8` and the inner VLAN 200, and `sp-cloud` from VLAN 400 to a service profile. VLAN 150 of `fp-a` has no circuit. `rsim-functions/src/circuit.rs` holds `Circuit` and `TagStack`. Each Fabric port has a port gate, an `AclMatch` with one rule for the tags of each circuit and a last unhandled rule, and a `Circuit`: a frame with exactly the tags of the near end of a circuit leaves the far end with the tags of that end, with an unknown layer 2 priority and the link delay in the missing list, or goes to the sink of the far end; each other frame is `Unknown`. The sink of `sp-cloud` is an `AclMatch` with one unhandled rule that names the service profile and the provider. `TagStack` pushes or pops one VLAN tag; the account pushes the outer tag 300 on the frames from port 2 of `sw-b` at the entry of `fp-b2` and pops it from the frames that leave `fp-b2` to that port.
